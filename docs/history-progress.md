# History and progress (Phase 4)

History searches the signed-in user's conversation titles, original questions, saved attempts, follow-up questions and delivered answers. Search is a literal substring, with protocol and status filters and server-side pagination. Invalidated events do not appear in search or counts. Titles are derived from the first question without AI; a participant can rename or reset their own chat, including a completed chat. Titles are display metadata and do not rewrite the original research question.

Progress defaults to everyday chats. Guided study data has its own view. Counts describe saved behavior, not intelligence, dependence, correctness or time spent thinking. Completion labels distinguish completed work, explicitly abandoned work, and open conversations; completion is self-reported. All-time totals and recent weekly activity are labeled separately. Opening history, progress or a conversation does not request AI.

Each chat has a factual activity summary and an optional AI review. Only clicking Generate AI review authorizes generation. Reviews use the existing daily allowance, shared token/dollar reservations, timeouts and provider fallback. A review uses bounded excerpts and recorded counts, identifies missing evidence, and cannot grade cognitive ability. Its source fingerprint marks whether newer activity exists; a completed review of unchanged activity is reused without another charge. Review events are separate from answer events, so reviews do not count as hints, trigger practice invitations or change research verification rates. Reviews are available on completed chats without reopening them; legacy guided sessions cannot request them.

Pending review IDs survive reload and transport retries. Reload only checks a previously requested review; it never starts a fresh one. Unknown interrupted usage keeps its reservation. Search, title edits, summaries and counters use no model calls. All reads and writes remain scoped to the authenticated participant.
